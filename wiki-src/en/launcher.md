# Shelf and launcher

## The shelf

The shelf holds GNOME's own indicators, so everything that shows up in GNOME's top bar works here.

- **Position**: bottom, left, right or top — *Settings → Shelf and launcher*.
- **Style**: across the whole edge or floating, with its own size, icon size, opacity and blur.
- **Apps**: drag icons to reorder, right-click to pin or unpin. A dot shows running apps; clicking an
  app with several windows cycles through them.
- **Auto-hide** hides the shelf under maximised windows.

## The launcher

Press <kbd>Super</kbd> or click the circle. Start typing to search:

- **apps**, **settings** and **files** in your home folder;
- **sums** — `12*(3+4)`, `2^10`, `200*15%`;
- **the web** — anything else goes to your search engine;
- **recent files** appear under “Continue where you left off”.

Choose what it searches and the search engine in *Settings → Shelf and launcher*. The launcher comes
as a bubble or full screen.

## Commands

Type a command and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Russian words work too.

| You type | What happens |
|---|---|
| `dark`, `light` | switch dark style |
| `dnd`, `do not disturb` | Do Not Disturb on or off |
| `night light` | Night Light on or off |
| `wifi`, `bluetooth` | turn the radio on or off |
| `volume 40` | set volume to 40 % |
| `brightness 70` | set screen brightness |
| `timer 5`, `timer 30 sec`, `timer 1 h` | a timer that rings when done |
| `coffee 30` | keep the screen on for 30 minutes |
| `lock`, `screenshot` | lock the screen, take a screenshot |
| `sleep`, `log out`, `restart`, `shut down` | power actions |
| `empty trash` | empty the trash |
| `mode`, `mode game` | turn a [focus mode](modes.html) on or off |
| `save session`, `restore session` | save or reopen your windows |

## Asking the AI

When the [assistant](assistant.html) is on, an **Ask Claude** button (named after your provider) appears next to the search results: it
sends what you typed to the assistant.
