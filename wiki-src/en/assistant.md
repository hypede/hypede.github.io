# AI assistant

The assistant opens as a panel on the right edge of the screen, like on a Chromebook.

## Setting it up

1. *Settings → AI assistant* → turn it on.
2. Pick a provider: Claude, Gemini, Mistral, ChatGPT, Grok or DeepSeek.
3. **Sign in** opens the provider's own page: you use your own account. HypeDE never sees or stores
   your password and uses no API keys.

## Where to ask

- the **Ask Claude** button (named after your provider) in the launcher sends what you typed;
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> sends the selected text from any app;
- in Files, **Ask** sends the selected files;
- **Ask Claude** (or your provider) in the title bar of Settings asks about a setting.

## Customizing HypeDE

The assistant can change HypeDE for you: style, accent color, wallpaper, the shelf (side, hiding,
floating), the launcher, animations, window corners, the clock, night light, mouse and touchpad.

- Ask in the chat, for example “dark style and the shelf on the left”, or press the gear button
  **Customize HypeDE** in the assistant panel.
- The assistant answers with a block of settings, and an **Apply in HypeDE** button appears under it.
- Nothing changes until you confirm in a window that lists every change as “old → new”. Afterwards
  **Undo** puts everything back.
- Simple requests (“dark style”, “night light”, “bigger text”) HypeDE understands by itself — this also
  works with the local model and without the internet.

Turn it off in *Settings → AI assistant → Let the assistant change HypeDE settings*.

## Privacy

The assistant only chats. It cannot run commands or read files on its own, and changes settings only
after you confirm; it sees only what you send it. It is off until you turn it on.
