A Chrome OS–style desktop for Linux

HypeDE installs next to GNOME as a separate session. You get the shelf, launcher, quick settings and lock screen of Chrome OS, with a regular GNOME Shell and all its apps underneath.

Install Source code

Shelf and launcher

Put the shelf at the bottom, side or top, stretch it across the screen or let it float. Drag icons to reorder them; windows minimize into their own icon.

The launcher finds apps, settings and files in your home folder, does arithmetic and sends anything else to your search engine. It comes compact or full screen.

Quick settings

Network, Bluetooth, sound, brightness, Night Light and Do Not Disturb as round buttons in three columns, like Chrome OS. They are GNOME's own toggles, so toggles added by apps work too.

Settings

One window for everything. Network, Bluetooth, sound, printers and users are KDE modules shown inside the page. Displays, mouse, keyboard, appearance, shelf and lock screen are HypeDE's own pages.

HypeDE keeps its settings apart from regular GNOME and can back them up to a file.

AI assistant

Pick Claude, Gemini, Mistral, ChatGPT, Grok or DeepSeek and sign in with your own account. The assistant opens as a panel on the right. “Ask” buttons live in the launcher, Files and Settings; from Files you can send the selected files.

It only chats: it cannot run commands and sees only what you send it. It is off until you turn it on.

Files

Sidebar and path bar like GNOME Files, tabs and a details pane like COSMIC Files. Thumbnails, drag and drop, background copying with undo, and a trash you can restore from.

Lock screen

A large clock, battery and load cards, and accent-coloured waves when you lock. Works with any login screen — GDM, SDDM, greetd or LightDM — with the password checked through PAM.

Install

Arch Linux, CachyOS and derivatives, with GNOME Shell 48 or newer.

git clone https://github.com/hypede/hypede
cd hypede/packaging
makepkg -si

Then log out and pick the HypeDE session on the login screen. KDE modules for Settings are optional:

sudo pacman -S plasma-nm bluedevil plasma-pa print-manager plasma-workspace \
               plasma-desktop kde-cli-tools flatpak-kcm kinfocenter
  • Replaces nothingNo GNOME package is modified; the regular GNOME session stays.
  • Separate settingsHypeDE has its own database: ~/.config/dconf/hypede.
  • Lite modeTurns off blur and extra effects on slower computers.
  • Free softwareGPL-3.0. Wallpapers and sounds are made for HypeDE.
