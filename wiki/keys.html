    Keys and gestures

    Keyboard

    Shortcut Action
    Super open or close the launcher
    Super+S window overview
    Super+L lock the screen
    Super+1…9 open the n-th app on the shelf
    Super+Space next keyboard layout
    Super+V clipboard history
    Super+Z float the window: small, on top of others; again to put it back
    Super+Shift+T reopen the window you just closed, in the same place
    Super+A ask the AI about the selected text
    Print Screen screenshot

    All of them can be changed in Settings → Device → Keyboard shortcuts. What Super opens (launcher or overview) is in Settings → Shelf and launcher.

    Touchpad

    Gesture Action
    Four fingers up open the launcher — or bring back windows hidden by the gesture below
    Four fingers down show the desktop: windows slide away and minimise
    Three fingers up window overview (GNOME)
    Three fingers left or right switch desks (GNOME)

    Gestures follow your fingers one-to-one. Let go before about a third of the way and everything goes back. Four-finger gestures can be switched off in Settings → Device → Touchpad.

    Mouse

    • Drag a window to a screen edge to snap it to half the screen, to the top to maximise.
    • Right-click an app on the shelf for its windows and actions.
    • Right-click a desktop widget to add, move back or remove widgets.
