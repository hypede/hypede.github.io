    VPN

    Settings → Network → Add VPN…:

    • Import a file… — the file your VPN provider gave you: WireGuard .conf, OpenVPN .ovpn, Cisco .pcf and others. OpenVPN and Cisco need the NetworkManager plugin (networkmanager-openvpn, networkmanager-vpnc); WireGuard works without plugins.
    • Paste WireGuard settings… — paste the [Interface] and [Peer] text and give it a name.

    Turn a VPN on with its switch or in quick settings. Click a VPN for Connect automatically and Remove.

    Search

    Type in the search field at the top: it finds pages and individual settings, in English and Russian.

    Backup

    System preferences → HypeDE configuration saves all HypeDE settings to a file and restores them. HypeDE's settings live in their own database, ~/.config/dconf/hypede, separate from regular GNOME.
