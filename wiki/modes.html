    Focus modes

    A focus mode switches several things at once and puts them back when you turn it off.

    Mode By default
    Work Do Not Disturb, balanced power
    Game Do Not Disturb, performance power, opens Steam if installed
    Study Do Not Disturb, balanced power, Lagoon theme

    Turning a mode on

    • the Focus tile in quick settings — its arrow lists the modes;
    • or type mode, mode game in the launcher.

    Editing modes

    Settings → System preferences → Focus modes. For each mode: name, Do Not Disturb, power mode, theme and apps to open. Add your own with New focus mode.

    What gets restored

    When a mode turns off, HypeDE restores Do Not Disturb, the power profile and the whole look as it was before (the previous theme is saved to ~/.local/state/hypede/). Apps the mode opened stay open.
