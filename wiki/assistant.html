    AI assistant

    The assistant opens as a panel on the right edge of the screen, like on a Chromebook.

    Setting it up

    1. Settings → AI assistant → turn it on.
    2. Pick a provider: Claude, Gemini, Mistral, ChatGPT, Grok or DeepSeek.
    3. Sign in opens the provider's own page: you use your own account. HypeDE never sees or stores your password and uses no API keys.

    Where to ask

    • the Ask Claude button (named after your provider) in the launcher sends what you typed;
    • Super+A sends the selected text from any app;
    • in Files, Ask sends the selected files;
    • Ask Claude (or your provider) in the title bar of Settings asks about a setting.

    Privacy

    The assistant only chats. It cannot run commands, change settings or read files on its own; it sees only what you send it. It is off until you turn it on.
