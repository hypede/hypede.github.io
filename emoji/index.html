How to use them

  • Discord: Server Settings › Emoji › Upload, pick files from png/128.
  • Telegram: send the files from png/512 to @Stickers as a new sticker set.
  • Websites and apps: use the SVGs from svg/; they scale to any size.

When you share or change them, keep the license: the GPL-3.0-or-later text is in the archive. Click an emoji to open its SVG.
